# Reference — panel discussion evidence base

This file anchors the `panel` / `five-role` workflow in published research. Use it when updating norms or explaining why the chair runs phased discussion.

## Core design principles

| Principle | Practical rule in this skill | Key sources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hybrid brainstorming | Phase 1 independent → Phase 2 exchange → Phase 3 refine | Korde & Paulus (2017); Girotra et al. (2010) |
| Autonomy-supportive facilitation | Chair invites, asks, withholds early verdict | Byron et al. (2022); Kundi et al. (2023) |
| Psychological safety | Dissent is information, not attack; model vulnerability | Edmondson (1999); O'Donovan et al. (2020) |
| Authentic dissent | Real minority concerns beat performative contrarianism | De Dreu & West (2001); DiPierro et al. (2021) |
| Hidden-profile surfacing | Phase 2 must expose unshared critical information | Schulz-Hardt et al. (2006) |
| Groups refine better than they invent | Phase 3 improves surviving options | McMahon et al. (2016) |
| Avoid herding | Chair probes non-convergent risks | Pick et al. (2024) |
| No participation gamification | Do not optimize for equal talk time | Hong et al. (2026) |

## Chair behavior checklist

Before Phase 1:

- [ ] Packet written: claim, decision, boundaries, evidence, success/failure
- [ ] Norms visible: independent first pass, no early verdict, evidence labels
- [ ] Chair preference withheld

During Phase 2–3:

- [ ] Ask validation/reflection questions
- [ ] Track shared assumptions and hidden profiles
- [ ] Limit scope to top 1–2 surviving options in refinement

Before final verdict:

- [ ] Second-chance prompt for minority objections
- [ ] Retain well-supported dissent
- [ ] Hand exactly one action to executor logic

## Consensus queries used for v1.2.0

1. `What team characteristics and discussion practices lead to more creative ideas and innovation in group brainstorming?`
2. `How should a discussion facilitator or chair behave to improve team decision quality, psychological safety, and avoid groupthink?`
